1. Overview & Platform Scope

Welcome to EventOps AI ("we", "our", or "the Platform"). EventOps AI is an autonomous, multi-agent event operations intelligence system that orchestrates conferences, hackathons, keynotes, registrations, and logistics.

We take privacy, compliance, and responsible AI governance seriously. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning personal data collected from registered attendees, keynote speakers, venue managers, sponsors, and event organizers.

2. Information We Collect
User & Account Data

Name, email address, secure hashed credentials, and role privileges (Attendee or Platform Administrator).

Registration Details

Event enrollment selections, ticket tiers (Standard, VIP, Speaker, Press), check-in logs, and timestamps.

Assistant Interactions

User queries made to the EventOps Assistant, reminder parameters, and session inquiries.

Feedback & Communications

Star ratings, feedback reviews, contact inquiries, and incident report tickets.

3. Multi-Agent Autonomous Processing

EventOps AI utilizes dedicated autonomous sub-agents to monitor operational health and attendee logistics:

  • Registration Agent: Ensures attendee quotas, issues tickets, and records attendance check-ins without sharing your data with unauthorized third parties.
  • Venue Agent: Aggregates room capacity and foot-traffic utilization strictly in anonymized, numerical format.
  • Speaker & Sponsor Agents: Manages public session agendas and sponsor deliverables committed to the platform.
  • Incident Agent: Collects triage tickets to quickly address network outages or logistics issues during live events.
4. Gemini AI & Machine Learning Ethics

Our conversational assistant integrates Google Gemini models to assist you with scheduling, queries, and navigation:

Zero Training on Personal Identifiers: Your private identity and user records are never used to train public language models. Queries are processed with zero-retention parameters, and queries for personal registrations resolve locally on our authenticated server.

The assistant will never prompt you for passwords, API keys, or financial credentials.

5. Storage, Encryption & Security

We employ enterprise-grade defense-in-depth principles:

  • Password Security: Passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are hashed using salted cryptographic algorithms (PBKDF2/Werkzeug).
  • Session Safeguards: HTTP sessions are managed through cryptographically signed cookies with anti-tampering verification.
  • Access Controls: Administrative endpoints are protected with strict role-based access control (RBAC).
